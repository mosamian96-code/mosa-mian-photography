2026-08-20 — Bug fix, round 2 (the real fix): diagnosed by downloading a real uploaded CR3 and inspecting it directly with exiftool (scripts/diagnose-orientation.ts, since removed). Found: RAW container Orientation=8, but the extracted JpgFromRaw preview's own Orientation tag is `undefined` -- the rotation info lives only on the RAW file's own EXIF, not on the embedded preview exiftool pulls out of it. `.rotate()` with no args only reads whatever orientation tag is on the buffer it's given, so on the preview buffer specifically it was silently a no-op the whole time; plain JPEG/HEIC uploads were never affected (their own file's EXIF carries a real tag).
